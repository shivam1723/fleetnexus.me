FAQ

Questions buyers ask first.

What is FleetNexus ERP software used for?

FleetNexus is an all-in-one ERP for small and mid-size manufacturing and waste management companies. It connects HR, CRM, sales, purchase, stock, production, logistics tracking, and accounting in one system.

Can FleetNexus be customized for our workflows?

Yes. Customization on request is the core difference. The product is shaped around how your teams move material, people, and money — not a rigid one-size template.

Does FleetNexus include fleet and logistics tracking?

Yes. Logistics sits in the same product as sales and inventory — vehicles, drivers, trips, and live geo location tracking.

Who is FleetNexus best suited for?

Small to mid-scale manufacturers and waste management operators that need HR through delivery and finance in one interconnected ERP.
